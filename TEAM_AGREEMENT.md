# Team Copper - Ownership Agreement

Not legal advice - a starting point for the six of us to read, discuss, and
adjust before treating it as settled. If GradPace turns into something we
actually pursue outside of CS411W, get an actual lawyer to review this before
relying on it.

## Who this covers

Stephen Steyaert, Dawson Fromm, Aaron Killingbeck, Carlycia Mathis, JJ Wile,
and Nate Balcha ("the team") - everyone listed as a member in `README.md`.

## Ownership

Copyright in GradPace (this repository's code, designs, and related
materials) is held **jointly and equally** by the team, regardless of how
much any individual member contributed, or when they joined or left the
project during CS411W.

## What requires everyone's agreement

No individual member (or subset of members) may, without written agreement
from the rest of the team:

- Grant a license to GradPace, or any part of it, to a third party
- Sell, assign, or transfer ownership or rights in GradPace
- Use GradPace, or code derived from it, in another commercial product
- Publish GradPace publicly under an open-source or other license

## If GradPace is pursued after the course ends

This agreement does not by itself set up a company, split equity, or divide
future revenue - those need a real agreement (e.g. an LLC operating
agreement) before any money or users are involved. What this agreement does
guarantee in the meantime: nobody can unilaterally take the project, license
it away, or cut another member out, before that real agreement exists.

## Changes

Any change to this agreement, or to who's covered by it, needs sign-off from
every current team member - recorded as a commit to this file that everyone
has agreed to (e.g. each member approving the PR that changes it).
